abstract: 'We develop a statistical explanation for why heavy-tailed spectra of neural network weight matrices predict model quality. A probabilistic framework yields a marginal likelihood computed from final-layer weights alone, which predicts performance across convolutional networks and large language models. For spectra following the High-Temperature Marchenko-Pastur distribution, we derive a limiting log-marginal likelihood and an optimal tail heaviness that empirically predicts overfitting.'
